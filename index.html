Welcome to Krishi AI

Krishi is an AI-powered multilingual IVR system designed for farmers who prefer voice-based guidance over smartphones. Farmers can simply call and receive real-time agricultural assistance including crop advice, weather updates, market prices, and fertilizer guidance. Our voice-based system breaks language and literacy barriers, bringing intelligent agricultural support directly through a phone call.

How Krishi AI IVR Works

  • Interactive Demo: This is a live simulation of our IVR system.
  • Phone Keypad: Click on dial pad numbers (0-9, *, #) to simulate a farmer pressing buttons on their phone.
  • AI Responses: The Krishi AI bot responds intelligently to each selection.
  • Chat Interface: Watch the conversation flow in real-time like a phone call conversation.
  • Multiple Languages: The system supports Hindi, English, and Malayalam.
  • Voice-Based: All responses are spoken aloud via text-to-speech (TTS) technology.
